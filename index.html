THE CHALLENGE

Campus should be easy to navigate.

Students with mobility and visual challenges can face barriers when travelling between campus buildings.

🪜

Stairs and steps

Wheelchair users may not be able to use stair-only pathways.

🛗

Unknown lifts and ramps

Students may not know where accessible entrances and lifts are located.

🧭

Finding classrooms

Unfamiliar buildings and unclear directions can make navigation difficult.

⚠️

Lift problems

Students need to know when a lift is unavailable before choosing a route.

A campus without barriers 💚
OUR SOLUTION

Technology that makes campus inclusive.

PathAble is a campus navigation concept that helps students choose suitable routes and receive understandable directions.

  • ✓ Fastest and step-free route choices
  • ✓ Voice-guided navigation
  • ✓ Directions using familiar landmarks
  • ✓ Lift issue reporting and verification demo
  • ✓ Alternative routes when a lift is unavailable
