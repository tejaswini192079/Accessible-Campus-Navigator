Our aim

Travelling around campus should be easy, safe and independent for everyone.

Whether you use a wheelchair, have low vision, or just want the quickest way to class, the app shows the right path for you.

The problem

Students don't know which path is safe and accessible for them

Every day, students with disabilities face avoidable barriers on campus.

01

Stairs block the way

Wheelchair users cannot easily use stairs and often take long detours.

02

Ramps & lifts are hidden

Students don't know where ramps and lifts are available.

03

Classrooms are hard to find

Visually impaired students may find it difficult to find classrooms.

04

Lifts stop working

Sometimes lifts break down, and students don't know about it until they arrive.

Our solution

A mobile app that shows the correct path

We are developing a mobile application that shows students the correct path to reach their destination.

    Main features

    Everything students need, in one app

    1

    Two route options

    Students can select the fastest route or a step-free route.

    • Fastest: shortest walking time
    • Step-free: avoids every staircase
    Try it →
    2

    Voice navigation

    The app speaks directions so visually impaired students can follow them. You can also speak a destination and the app plans the route.

    • Read-aloud, next step and stop controls
    • Voice commands for hands-free use
    Try voice →
    3

    Landmark directions

    The app gives directions using familiar places like the main gate, reception and lift.

    • "Look for the glass entrance and help desk"
    • Easy to follow without looking at a map
    See directions →
    4

    Report broken lift

    Students can inform others when a lift is not working, with a short note or a recorded voice message.

    • Report in seconds
    • Other students see the warning
    Report a lift →
    5

    Route update

    If a lift is broken, the app finds another accessible route after the issue is verified.

    • Automatic re-routing around the broken lift
    • Back to normal once it is fixed
    See lift status →
