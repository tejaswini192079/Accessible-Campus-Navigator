Our aim

To make travelling around campus easy, safe and independent for everyone — whether you use a wheelchair, have low vision, or simply want the quickest way to class.

The Problem

Students don't know which path is safe and accessible for them

Every day, students with disabilities face avoidable barriers on campus.

🪜

Stairs everywhere

Wheelchair users cannot easily use stairs and often take long detours.

🛗

Hidden ramps & lifts

Students don't know where ramps and lifts are available on campus.

🔎

Hard to find rooms

Visually impaired students may find it difficult to locate classrooms.

⛔

Broken lifts

Lifts sometimes stop working and nobody knows until they arrive.

Our Solution

A mobile app that shows the correct path

Choose a destination, pick a route type, and the app guides you step by step.

⚡

Fastest route

The route that takes the least time.

♿

Step-free route

A route without stairs, using ramps and lifts.

🔊

Voice directions

The app tells students where to go, out loud.

🏛️

Landmark directions

Instructions using reception, lifts and entrances.

📢

Report broken lift

Students can report when a lift isn't working.

🔄

Route updates

A new route is suggested once a lift issue is verified.
